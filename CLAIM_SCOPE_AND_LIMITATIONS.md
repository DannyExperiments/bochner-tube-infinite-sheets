# Claim scope and limitations

Both parts of Hazra’s Open Question 2.3 have negative answers. There is a domain in complex dimension two with a centered round disk tube type and infinitely many points of its envelope over each point of one projected circle.

The smooth connected boundary belongs to the real disk tube type. The result is in complex dimension two and makes no claim about global smoothness of the domain boundary or the full envelope geometry.

The literature search is bounded. No proof-assistant formalization or independent peer review is claimed.
