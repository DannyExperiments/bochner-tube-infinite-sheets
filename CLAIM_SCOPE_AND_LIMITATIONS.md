# Claim scope and limitations

Both parts of Hazra’s Open Question 2.3 have negative answers. There is a domain in complex dimension two with a centered round disk tube type and infinitely many points of its envelope over each point of one projected circle.

The smooth connected boundary belongs to the real disk tube type. The result is in complex dimension two and makes no claim about global smoothness of the domain boundary or the full envelope geometry.

The literature search is bounded. External peer review is pending.

The quadratic separating polynomial factors on the affine seed; the second factor has positive real part along the circle. The explicit logarithmic trace exponentiates to the polynomial factor expression, and its values after successive full turns differ by multiples of 2πi and are pairwise distinct. The construction, continuation and identification of these lifts inside the envelope of holomorphy, including gluing the full exterior, are in the manuscript.
